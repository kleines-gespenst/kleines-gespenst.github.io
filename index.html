About

I am a PhD researcher at TU Wien, advised by Terra Blevins and Allan Hanbury. I am also currently a SPAR fellow, working on model diffing.

I work on how language models acquire natural language, how that knowledge is stored, and whether different types of knowledge (e.g. factual and linguistic) are stored in different ways, including in multilingual settings that go beyond high-resource languages.

Mechanistic interpretability · Model diffing · Multilingual NLP · Language modeling · AI safety

Education

  • PhD in Computer Science, TU Wien, 2023 to expected autumn 2027. Thesis: “Factual and Linguistic Knowledge in Language Models”.
  • MA in Byzantine and Modern Greek Studies, University of Vienna, 2022. Thesis.
  • BA in Linguistics, Saint Petersburg State University, 2014.

Teaching
